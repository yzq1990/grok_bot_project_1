# Health check (Grok Bot)

Run when asked to audit, close a phase, or assess handoff need. Do **not** wake every idle agent.

## Check

1. Role index vs live teammates  
2. Deliverables missing a sole owner  
3. Status words collapsed or hearsay auth  
4. Stale TODOs without blockers named  
5. Routines that may double-dispatch  

## Report

Only **new** issues, coverage gaps, and next owner. Ordinary task work does not require a full-project audit.
